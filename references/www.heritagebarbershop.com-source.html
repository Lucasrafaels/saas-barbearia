SOUTHEAST PORTLAND’S CLASSIC BARBERSHOP EXPERIENCE

 

Heritage Barbershop offers a traditional barbershop experience, specializing in the classic art of styling and straight razor shaving. Located in Southeast Portland on the border of the Buckman and Kerns neighborhoods, Heritage Barbershop will transport you to an authentic traditional barbershop experience. Family owned and operated, our experienced barbers provide top-notch service with a dedication to making you look and feel your best in a setting that’s comfortable for the whole family.
