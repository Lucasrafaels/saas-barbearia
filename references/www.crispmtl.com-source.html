7 YEARS OF CRISP.

BOOK NOW
Book Now

CRISP is a modern-day barbershop that fuses traditional barbering with contemporary men’s hairstyling. Since opening in 2018, it has become a must-visit destination in Montreal, redefining the standards of men’s grooming. With locations in Pointe-Saint-Charles and the Village, CRISP has built a loyal clientele, welcoming over 30,000 clients in recent years.
